What we're known for

Four things we make every single day, brewed and cooked fresh at the counter.

Chai

Indian Snacks

Samosas, pakoda, vada and rolls, fried fresh through the day.

Paratha

About Munna Bhai

Munna Bhai Coffee Shop is a neighbourhood chai and snacks counter in Dubai, serving karak chai, Indian snacks, dosa, paratha and curries throughout the day.

Drop in for a quick cup of chai on the go, or settle in for a full plate — we're open long hours to catch you whenever the craving hits.
